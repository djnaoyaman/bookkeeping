# Book Keeping — Tricolore

Palette: deep blue `#16366b`, warm white `#fffdf9`, white `#ffffff`, red `#bd2637`, pale blue `#edf2fa`. Japanese text remains clear and functional; serif Latin branding, quiet rules, white space and restrained red accents provide the French editorial direction.

The original BK vector monogram is white on blue with a red underline. Buttons and correct states use blue; incorrect states use red with a pale background. Body text stays deep blue on white rather than red on blue.

`favicon.svg` is the vector source generated by `scripts/brand_assets.py`. Apple Touch Icons have opaque blue backgrounds. Maskable glyphs remain in the safe area. Social images have a three-part blue/white/red rule, serif wordmark and the same BK mark.

Open Graph images are 1200×630; the main X card is 1200×600. Favicon, Apple icons, image metadata and stylesheet URLs use the `tricolore-1` version marker to refresh caches. Actual social previews are still subject to platform caching and crop rules.
