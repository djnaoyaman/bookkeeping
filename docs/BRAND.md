# Book Keeping visual assets

Palette: paper `#f5f4ee`, ink `#242621`, lime `#d9f458`. The BK monogram is drawn as vector geometry and remains independent of installed fonts. The book-like B and directional K form a compact mark for tabs and home screens.

`favicon.svg` is the vector source. `scripts/brand_assets.py` renders PNG and ICO variants and the original social-card designs. Apple Touch Icons have an opaque ink background. The maskable icon keeps the monogram within the central safe area. Social-card text and geometry stay away from edge crops.

Assets: `assets/og-bookkeeping.png` (1200×630), `assets/card-bookkeeping.png` (1200×600), exam-specific `assets/og-{nissho,zenkei,uscpa}.png`, `assets/og-author.png`, and the icon variants referenced by the HTML/manifest.

Do not stretch, add shadows, change the palette per platform, or reproduce third-party logos. The sharing images are design assets, not platform-specific screenshot verification. Actual SNS previews remain subject to platform caching, crop rules and crawler access.
